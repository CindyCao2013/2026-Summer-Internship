# Intraday Alpha OOS v1

## Frozen research contract

The OOS run is governed by
`research/config/intraday_alpha_freeze_v1.json`, SHA-256
`c4d09c12952cdcf3d58b3bef3b32ced04d0843205ca3a73fc0f8d8356fc70bc3`.
The specification freezes all 12 primary factors and, for each factor, its
execution bartime, return horizon, direction, portfolio rule, residual control
set, and residual direction. `large_active_buy_ratio` is locked out as
`proxy_only`.

Direction is the sign of the 2024H1 raw IC. OOS raw IC and raw high-minus-low
returns are multiplied by that frozen direction. The runner never infers
direction from OOS H-L returns and contains no best-slot or best-horizon search.
Non-frozen horizons are exported as decay diagnostics only.

## Data periods

- Discovery/train: 2024-01-01 through 2024-06-30.
- Validation: 2024-07-01 through 2024-12-31, 125 evaluation dates for most
  factors.
- Test: 2025-01-01 through 2025-08-18, 152 evaluation dates for most factors.

The 2025 test is explicitly `test_2025_available`, not a full calendar year,
because the audited `Stock_one_minute` table ends on 2025-08-18.

## OOS conclusions

### Retain

- `close_vwap_deviation`: signed ICIR 3.32 in 2024H2 and 5.73 in 2025
  available; fixed-direction H-L Sharpe 2.10 and 3.44.
- `intraday_amihud`: signed ICIR 7.72 and 6.19; residual signed ICIR 6.79 and
  6.25. The liquidity signal is highly stable.
- `realized_volatility`: signed ICIR 10.02 and 10.58; residual signed ICIR
  11.66 and 11.53.
- `minute_skew`: signed ICIR 9.81 and 9.52; residual signed ICIR 11.18 and
  9.45. It retains independent distribution information.

### Watch

- `volume_front_loading` and `volume_back_loading` preserve the frozen IC sign,
  but their fixed-direction H-L portfolios are unstable or negative.
- `active_buy_sell_imbalance` strengthens in 2025, but 2024H2 H-L Sharpe is
  only 0.53.
- `bartime_ofi` preserves raw and residual IC direction in both periods, while
  fixed-direction H-L Sharpe declines from 1.66 to 0.83.
- `active_buy_shock` decays sharply in 2025: signed ICIR falls to 0.55 and
  residual signed ICIR to 0.11.
- `average_active_trade_size` retains raw and residual IC, but the H-L
  portfolio is inconsistent across the two periods.

### Drop

- `late_session_strength`: the frozen positive IC direction reverses in the
  2025 available period; raw ICIR is -0.22.
- `ofi_persistence`: the raw signal remains predictive, but its frozen
  production-control residual reverses in 2024H2. It fails the incremental
  alpha test even though raw performance later recovers.

`retain` means signal survival under the frozen tuple. It is not equivalent to
production approval. Cost conclusions in the original OOS output were based on
the fixed `turnover_b_hl=4` display model and are now explicitly labeled
legacy. Position-level cost results are recorded in
`research/docs/intraday_portfolio_cost_v1.md`.

## Reproducibility and safeguards

- Freeze creator/verifier: `research/freeze_intraday_alpha_v1.py`
- Frozen specification: `research/config/intraday_alpha_freeze_v1.json`
- OOS runner: `research/run_intraday_alpha_oos_v1.py`
- Contract tests: `tests/test_intraday_alpha_oos_v1.py`
- Main comparison:
  `research/results/intraday_alpha_oos_v1/intraday_alpha_oos_v1.csv`
- Long-form metrics:
  `research/results/intraday_alpha_oos_v1/oos_metrics_long.csv`
- Diagnostic decay:
  `research/results/intraday_alpha_oos_v1/oos_ic_decay_diagnostic.csv`
- Position-level cost simulation:
  `research/results/intraday_portfolio_simulator_v1/intraday_portfolio_cost_v1.csv`

The 2025 factor queries are built in six-month chunks because a single
2025-01-01 through 2025-08-18 `ofi_persistence` query exceeded DolphinDB server
memory. Per-period checkpoints are tied to the freeze hash and make retries
idempotent.

No change was made to `Intraday_Factor_Test_Process.py`, `intraday_lib.py`, or
the portfolio engine.

## Sprint 4.2.3 metric normalization

The v1 OOS conclusions remain frozen research evidence. Canonical
cross-sectional reporting is now regenerated by
`research/run_intraday_evaluation_v2.py` using the exact filtered-constituent
equal-weight benchmark. Decile metrics are explicit market-excess returns,
while direction-adjusted H-L retains the frozen tuple.

`hl_market_beta` and `hl_market_corr` are reported as diagnostics. The
algebraic cancellation of the benchmark from H-L does not imply beta
neutrality. Updated frozen diagnostics are written to
`research/results/intraday_evaluation_v2/frozen_oos_diagnostics_v2.csv`;
there is still no OOS tuple or direction re-selection.
